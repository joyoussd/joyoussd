# Hi, I’m Souvik 

PhD Researcher in **Magnetics & Power Electronics** at the University of Pittsburgh.

I work on **physics-guided modeling and design of magnetic components**, with the goal of turning complex material behavior into **clean, plug-and-play datasheets** that integrate seamlessly with design and optimization tools.

---

## 🔬 Current Research Focus
- Physics-based modeling of magnetic components for power electronics  
- Frequency-dependent permeability, loss, and variability modeling  
- EMI-aware component design and impedance behavior  
- FEM-driven design and multi-objective optimization

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Methods
- **FEM:** COMSOL Multiphysics, ANSYS Maxwell  
- **Modeling & Optimization:** MATLAB, Python  
- **Circuits & Systems:** LTspice, PLECS  
- **Design Philosophy:** Experiments ↔ Models ↔ Design Tools
